NPC, creature and ship stat blocks in wiki articles and adventure previews.

```jsx
<StatBlock name="Toll Captain" kind="Medium humanoid (dragonborn), lawful neutral"
  abilities={[{label:"STR",value:"16 (+3)"}]} rows={[{label:"Armour Class",value:"15"}]}
  traits={[{name:"Right of Passage",text:"Ships that pay cannot be attacked…"}]} />
```

Keep the label/value grammar of the printed book. This is the one component allowed to use bold
sans inline, because a stat block is a table pretending to be prose.
