Long-form reading surface for field notes and wiki articles.

```jsx
<ReadingPanel meta="Design · 6 min read" title="Designing an economy out of a dead god"
  action={<Button variant="quiet" href="#notes">Continue reading →</Button>}>
  <p>The essence falls whether anyone is ready for it…</p>
</ReadingPanel>
```

Do NOT switch to the parchment theme on the site — a light band inside a dark site reads as a
mistake. Parchment (#F5F0E4 / #2A2636) is for printed pages and PDFs only.
