Site nav. Transparent over key art, solid navy with a hairline once scrolled.

```jsx
<SiteNav scrolled={scrolled} brand={<Wordmark />} links={NAV}
  cta={<Button size="sm" href="#signup">Newsletter</Button>} />
```

Labels are single words where possible (World, Wiki, Adventures, Shop, Tools, Gallery).
Never more than seven plus the CTA.
