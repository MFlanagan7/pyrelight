Tides of Marrow Bay

A toll ledger names three houses that should never have appeared on one page. Six to eight sessions on the Drowned Coast, for a party arriving at level one with nothing to lose.

Levels 1–4 6–8 sessions 64 pages

The hooks

Four ways in, so you can start from whatever the party already cares about. Each one lands them on the quay within the first twenty minutes.

GM material

Everything below is hidden by default so this page can sit on a shared screen. Players should stop reading here.

Act one — the week at the weighing house. The party sees the toll working as intended and meets everyone who will matter. One assay is wrong and nobody comments on it.

Act two — the ledger. The wrong assay leads to a page naming Marrow, Calder and the cartel in the same consignment. Act two is the party deciding who to hand it to, and every option costs something.

Act three — the spring renegotiation, early. Whoever the party gave the ledger to arrives at the bay with it. The arc ends with a new price for essence and at least one house owing the party a favour it resents.

The assayer on the quay filed the wrong number deliberately and wants to be caught, because being arrested by the Synod is safer than being found by whoever paid her. She will not say this until act two.

The toll captain the party will like most is on the cartel's payroll and has been for nine years. He is not a traitor to Marrow in his own account — he considers the arrangement to include him.

The ledger surfaces anyway in week three, found by a Synod clerk who does not survive the month. The renegotiation happens without the party and the price of essence rises nineteen per cent, which they will feel in every subsequent adventure. Nothing here requires the party to act; the coast simply becomes more expensive to live on.

A print-ready facsimile is included as a player handout at the back of the book. It names one consignment, three houses and a weight that does not match the assay filed the same day.

Hand it over physically if you can. The whole arc works better when the players are holding the evidence rather than being told about it.

What is inside

This arc deals with debt, drowning and coerced testimony. The session-zero tone sheet in the free downloads covers all three and how to dial each one down; the adventure text flags every scene where they appear.
