Cover art · The Starfall Setting

Marrow Bay at low tide

The flats east of the chain, an hour after low water, with the weighing house lit and the mudlark boats already out. This is the image the whole book was designed around.

The brief

We asked for a working port rather than a beautiful one: something that looks like a place where people are paid badly to do dangerous things at four in the morning. The instruction that mattered most was "the light is a commodity, not a miracle" — no glow, no rapture, just cold value being weighed under lamps.

Three revisions. The first had too much sky and read as fantasy landscape; the second moved the horizon down and put the boats in the foreground, which is where it found the subject.

An hour after low water, the flats are worth more than the town behind them.
