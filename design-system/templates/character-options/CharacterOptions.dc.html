No option here gives a character essence-based powers. Essence is money in this setting, not a spell list — a character who wants to use it has to buy it, carry it and spend it like everybody else. The Weighed background is about being marked by it, not empowered.

The two extra questions

Whatever you build, the quickstart asks two things at the end of character creation. They matter more than any subclass choice on this page.

One

Which house does your family owe, and for what?

Two

What is the most essence you have ever held at once?
