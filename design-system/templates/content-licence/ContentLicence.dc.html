What you may do with Starfall

Short version: run it, stream it, write for it, sell what you write. Do not resell our files or our art, and do not imply we are involved.

Version 1.2 Effective 14 Fallmoon
This licence is deliberately generous because a setting nobody is allowed to build on is a setting nobody uses. If something you want to do is not covered here, write to us and ask. The answer is usually yes, and if it is no we will tell you why.

Yes, freely

No permission needed, no credit required, no need to tell us. Go ahead.

Yes, with conditions

You may write and sell your own Starfall material — adventures, regions, houses, gods, supplements, tools. Three conditions, all of them about not confusing your readers.

"This work is unofficial fan content compatible with Starfall, published by Pyrelight Press. Not endorsed by or affiliated with Pyrelight Press."

Put it on the title page or the product listing. That single sentence satisfies all three conditions at once.

There is no revenue threshold and no royalty. If you sell ten thousand copies of a Starfall supplement, that money is yours. We would like a link.

No

These are the things that take money out of the studio's pocket or mislead someone into thinking they bought something official.

Who owns what

What you write is yours. A house you invent, an NPC you name, a port you put on the coast — you own that outright, and you do not assign it to us by publishing it as Starfall-compatible. We will not take your idea and put it in a book without asking and paying you.

What we published stays ours: the text of the books, the setting's own names and places, the wordmark, and the art we commissioned. Names like Marrow Bay, House Calder, the Synod and the Tidewalker are ours as trademarks and as copyright; you may write about them, and you may not claim them.

Starfall is compatible with fifth edition and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast. Rules content in our books is offered under the terms noted in each product; whatever licence covers the mechanics you are using still applies alongside this one.

If this licence changes

Anything you published under an earlier version stays licensed under that version. We will not revoke permission retroactively and make your back catalogue infringing — that is the whole point of putting a version number at the top. Previous versions stay archived and linked below.

We can withdraw permission from a specific person for a specific reason, in writing, and the only reason we ever have is the conduct clause above.
