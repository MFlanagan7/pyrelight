The Starfall Setting

The whole world in one book: four regions, twelve houses, seven gods who still answer, and the essence economy that binds them. Written for GMs who want a coastline with consequences.

Everything in the book has been run at open tables. The regional chapters carry tide tables, toll schedules and price lists you can put in front of players, and every GM-facing secret is marked as such so the book is safe to pass around.
