The Tidewalker

Stopped speaking to clergy ninety-one years ago and has communicated through the tide ever since. Stronger now than before the fall, which nobody in the Synod is willing to write down.

Ascendant Sea, measure, arrival 9 pages

Before the fall the Tidewalker was a minor coastal god with four temples and a reputation for punctuality. It was the god you prayed to for a tide that arrived when it said it would, which in a fishing economy is not a small thing.

The fall took the punctuality and gave back something much larger. The tides have not run to schedule since, and the Tidewalker's authority over the water has grown in exact proportion to how unpredictable that water has become. Its clergy find this theologically uncomfortable and its congregations do not care.

The silence

Ninety-one years ago the Tidewalker stopped answering clergy directly. No vision, no voice, no dream — and no explanation. What replaced it was the tide itself: intervals that resolve into answers if you keep a long enough record, which is why the god's priesthood is now largely a profession of statisticians.

A question put to the Tidewalker is answered over weeks. This makes it useless for emergencies and unusually trustworthy for everything else, and it has quietly become the god that merchants consult before signing.

Favour and its cost

Favour is earned by honest measure and lost by false measure — of cargo, of debt, of anything a person could lie about for gain. The god does not care about cruelty, piety or worship attendance. It cares whether the number you wrote down is the number that was true.

Clergy

Six hundred ordained, most of them working assayers, harbourmasters or tide clerks with a second title. The order has no central seat: the tide is the seat. This has made it impossible for any house to capture and is the reason the Synod finds it so irritating.

 A character with favour may put one question to the tide per season. The answer arrives across the following four weeks as a pattern in the tide table, and requires a Wisdom (Insight) or Intelligence (Investigation) check at DC 14 to read. The answer is always true and rarely timely.

Sites of worship
