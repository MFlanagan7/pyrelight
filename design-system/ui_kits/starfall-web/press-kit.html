One line

Starfall is a 5e-compatible fantasy setting where a dead god's remains fall from the sky as currency.

Short — 50 words

Starfall is a 5e-compatible fantasy setting from Pyrelight Press. A century ago something enormous came apart above the world; what is left falls as essence, burns cold, and can be spent. Twelve merchant houses, three cartels and one frightened Synod are all in the business of collecting it.

Studio

Pyrelight Press is an independent tabletop roleplaying studio. Starfall is its first published world.
